### How I work

- **Coding agents every day, with the architecture in my hands.** Plan first, small diffs, every
  diff reviewed before it lands. Fifteen products in twelve months is what that discipline buys.
- **I own what ships.** If it breaks in production it is mine to debug, and I can defend every
  decision in a review.
- **Product before code.** What to build, what to cut and what to monetize are part of the job, not
  someone else's problem.
